# Privacy policy

_Glyph Garden, version 0.3.0. Last updated 15 November 2026._

Glyph Garden is a new-tab page. It collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

## What it stores

Your Tune settings (the sliders, colours and your name for the greeting) and your chosen scene are
saved on your own device, using the browser's extension storage (the `storage` permission). They never
leave your device. Remove the extension, or clear its data, and they are gone.

## What it does not do

- It does not collect, record or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or usage statistics.
- It has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no advertising.
- It makes no network requests of its own. Its code is bundled in the extension, and it does not load
  or run any remote code.
- It does not read the pages you visit.

## The search box

When you search from the new tab, the text is passed to the search engine you already chose in your
browser, using the browser's `search` permission. Glyph Garden does not see the results and keeps no
copy of what you typed. What the search engine does with your query is governed by that search
engine's own policy.

## Permissions

- `storage`: to save your Tune settings on your device.
- `search`: to send what you type in the search box to your default search engine.

## Children

Glyph Garden collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this file in the repository, with a new
date at the top.
